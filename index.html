Powerful features to manage shared expenses

From splitting the rent with roommates to calculating costs for a weekend getaway, SplitMate handles the math so you don't have to.

Add & Split Bills

Add receipts instantly. Split expenses equally, by percentages, or exact amounts. Attach receipt photos for full transparency.

Integrated Group Chat

Discuss expenses right where they happen. Send messages, react to payments, and keep the conversation contextual.

Monthly Settlements

Perfect for roommates. Get automated monthly reports summarizing all shared living expenses and one-click settlements.

Balance Tracking

Always know "who owes who". Our smart algorithm minimizes the total number of transactions needed to settle all debts.

How it works

Start splitting expenses in seconds. No complicated sign-ups.

1

Create a Group

Download the Android app, enter a group name (like "Euro Trip"), and share the invite link with your friends.

2

Add Expenses

Whenever someone pays for something, just add it to the group. We calculate everyone's share automatically.

3

Settle Up

When you're ready, hit "Settle Up" to see the simplest way to pay everyone back. Record the payment and you're done!
