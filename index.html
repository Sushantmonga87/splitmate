How it works

Start splitting expenses in seconds. No complicated sign-ups. Get back to enjoying your time with friends.

1. Create a Group

Download the Android app, enter a group name (like "Euro Trip"), and share the invite link with your friends.

2. Add Expenses

Whenever someone pays for something, just add it to the group. Chat about it and let us calculate the shares.

3. Settle Up

Hit "Settle Up" to see the simplest way to pay everyone back. Record the payment and zero out your balances!
